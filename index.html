About Me

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering and am currently completing my M.Sc. in Industrial and Systems Engineering at the Federal University of Technology – Paraná (UTFPR), Brazil. I am also an active researcher on the OPTISUS-IA project, developing research on sustainable mobility transition and network optimization using machine-learning and artificial-intelligence models. As international experience, I took part in the Sandwich Program at the Shibaura Institute of Technology, Japan (2023), and the International Master Program at IPP-Porto, Portugal (2026).

During my bachelor's, my research focused on software automation for sustainable development and low-impact development (LID) technologies. This led me to explore smart cities and sustainable transportation during my master's, and my work now spans public transportation, data pipelines, sustainable development, and machine-learning models. I have mainly developed statistical and deep-learning models and software for the public-transit sector, working directly with real operator data from Brazil and Portugal. This work has produced several peer-reviewed conference papers — including one on forecasting public-transport demand for resilient and sustainable smart cities (20th SDEWES Conference, Dubrovnik) and one presenting a conceptual data-pipeline framework for public-transport forecasting (ICEE 2026, Porto) — as well as an open-source toolkit, PID-Transit, for GTFS–NeTEx interoperability, which I am currently preparing as a journal article for Public Transport (Springer).

On the OPTISUS-IA project, I collaborate across institutions in two countries: UTFPR and the University of Campinas (Unicamp) in Brazil, and the Polytechnic Institute of Porto and the University of Minho in Portugal. Funded by CNPq (the Brazilian National Council for Scientific and Technological Development), the project aims to develop an integrated artificial-intelligence tool for optimizing sustainable public-transport systems. It has opened new horizons for research on data standards for intermodality and public-transit management and regulation, and has given me the opportunity to build many connections and take part in numerous events.

Professionally, I work daily with Python and data analysis, building software for financial institutions in Brazil. This has given me experience with API management, big-data pipelines, and anonymization of sensitive data, including developing solutions that comply with ISO 27001 for data security. My most recent project applies machine learning to crop classification, using indices derived from Sentinel-1/2 satellite imagery to identify crop type, growth stage, and coverage for a given area.

Languages: Portuguese (native) · English (proficient) · Spanish (intermediate) · Mandarin Chinese (intermediate)

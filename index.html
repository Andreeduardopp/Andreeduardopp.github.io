01 · About

From civil engineering to software, data and AI

My journey in software development began during my undergraduate capstone project, where I automated the analysis of parameters related to permeable pavements. Subsequently, I joined SoftFocus, where I worked with Django and Angular to develop software solutions for Brazilian financial institutions, such as BB, BNB, and Santander, while expanding my scope to include backend systems, data pipelines, and artificial intelligence applications.

Alongside my professional experience, I conducted research and collaborated with international institutions during my Master’s program. This included participating in two research projects, one of which was an international initiative involving five institutions across Brazil and Portugal, and authoring papers presented at both national and international conferences.

My background combines software development, technical decision-making, and collaborative effort to build practical, scalable solutions.
